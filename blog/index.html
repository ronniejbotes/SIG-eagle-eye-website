What we write about.

Each article answers one question, for the person who runs the site, the shift or the business.

  • What Eagle Eye does

    One capability at a time: how a flag is raised, who reviews it, and what goes on the record.

  • What it doesn't do

    The limits, stated as plainly as the features. It doesn't decide who gets disciplined: a person reviews every flag, and management decides what happens next.

  • Your industry, your business

    The staff problems a factory, a fleet, a mine, a clinic, a store or a security operation actually deals with, one at a time.

  • Changes in the law

    New judgments, Codes of Good Practice and POPIA guidance that touch cameras, discipline or safety at work, dated and linked to the source.

How we write them

A legal point always names its source and links to it, so you can read the Act or the judgment for yourself. When an article discusses a case, it names the employer and the forum where that matters, and never an employee or any other individual.

The articles are general information for employers, not legal advice on your situation. For that, speak to a labour law professional before you act.
