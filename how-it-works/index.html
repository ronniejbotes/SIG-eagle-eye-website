How it works · Patent pending

Five stages. A person decides, every time.

SIG Eagle Eye's patent-pending method turns camera footage into something you can act on fairly: a classified, reviewed record that follows labour law. Here is exactly what happens, from the moment something is seen to the moment someone decides what to do about it.

01 · Detect

Behavioural analytics watch the zones you choose.

Configured for your site, the system notices behaviour worth a second look. It watches the zones you agreed, for the rules you agreed, and nothing else.

  • PPE and safe actsHard hats, vests, guards and walkways
  • Restricted zonesWho enters, when, and with what permit
  • Time at the work areaStations and posts left unattended
  • Stock and assetsHow goods, tools and fuel are handled
  • Vehicles and peopleForklifts, yards and loading bays
  • Flow and outputIdle stations and bottlenecks

Never toilets, change rooms or any space outside the agreed zones. A detection is only ever a reason to look closer.

02 · Classify

Sorted the way labour law sorts it.

Misconduct, poor performance and a safety lapse are handled differently in law. Get the category wrong and a fair outcome gets harder. So every event is classified against labour law and your own disciplinary code before a person sees it.

03 · Review

A person looks at every flag. You make this call.

This is the step the whole service is built around. A reviewer confirms the flag, adds the context the camera can't see, or dismisses it. Every decision is logged, and nothing moves forward without one.

  1. 1Reference and time. Every record can be traced back to the moment it happened.
  2. 2Where. Down to the zone configured for your site.
  3. 3What was observed. A plain description of behaviour, never a conclusion about a person.
  4. 4The rule. A rule the employee knew, or could reasonably be expected to know. The Dismissal Code asks exactly that.
  5. 5The category. Because misconduct, performance and safety follow different processes.
  6. 6The evidence. The footage that matters, kept with the record, not lost in hours of recordings.
  7. 7The reviewer's decision. Who looked, when, and what they decided.
  8. 8Who has seen it. Controlled access, with every opening logged.

05 · Act, lawfully

You decide what happens next, with a fair procedure.

Eagle Eye never disciplines anyone. It gives management what it needs to correct early and fairly, and to escalate only when a pattern continues.

  1. 01Counselling
  2. 02Verbal warning
  3. 03Written warning
  4. 04Final written warning
  5. 05Enquiry

Correct first, escalate if it continues. Very serious misconduct can go straight to an enquiry, and dismissal is reserved for when the working relationship has become intolerable.

A fair procedure, per the 2025 Dismissal Code

  • The allegation in writing, with enough detail to answer it
  • Enough time to prepare
  • A real chance to respond before any decision
  • Assistance from a fellow employee or a union representative
  • A language the employee understands, where reasonably possible
  • A record of the decision and the reasons for it

Operations managers ask

Process questions

All questions
Do we need new cameras?

Not necessarily. The site assessment starts with the coverage you already have and tells you honestly where it falls short.

Who reviews the flags?

Reviewers are agreed at setup, so review sits with people who know your rules. SIG Eagle Eye's labour law specialists support the process, and optional legal integration support is available when an incident moves into a formal procedure.

Can we choose what is and isn't monitored?

Yes. That is what configuration is. You choose the zones and the rules within what the law allows. Private spaces such as toilets and change rooms are always excluded.

How long are records kept?

Only as long as the purpose needs and your policy and the law require. After that they are deleted, in line with POPIA's rule on retention. See the compliance framework.

Does it work across several sites?

Yes. Rules are configured per site, and reporting can be seen site by site or across the whole group.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on the number of sites and how ready your policies are. We agree your installation date and the plan for any further sites with you when we speak.
