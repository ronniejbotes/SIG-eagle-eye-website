What SIG Eagle Eye does not do

All decisions remain human-led and legally guided.

  • It does not make automatic disciplinary decisions
  • It does not dismiss employees
  • It does not operate unlawfully or secretly
  • It does not replace management judgment
  • It does not remove procedural fairness requirements

That line matters in law as well as in principle: section 71 of POPIA protects people from decisions with legal consequences that are based solely on automated processing, including profiles of their performance at work and their conduct.

All frequently asked questions
Is it legal to monitor employees with cameras in South Africa?

Generally yes, in work areas, when employees are told about it, the purpose is legitimate and the information is handled in line with POPIA. Monitoring private spaces such as bathrooms and changing rooms is not acceptable. SIG Eagle Eye is structured around these requirements, including transparent notification and data minimisation. See our compliance framework.

Does the system decide who gets disciplined?

No. It does not make disciplinary decisions or dismiss anyone. It flags, classifies and documents. A person reviews every incident, and management decides what happens next, following fair procedure.

Will our employees know about it?

Yes. Transparent employee notification is one of the built-in safeguards. It does not operate secretly. Our page for employees explains it in plain language you can share with your team.
