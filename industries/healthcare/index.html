Healthcare facilities

Care for patients. Fairness for staff.

Hospitals, clinics and care homes run around the clock, on trust. SIG Eagle Eye helps you keep that trust by recording what happens at nurses' stations, medicine rooms and entrances, while patient spaces stay private by design.

Why healthcare is different

Three things no other workplace has at once.

That's why a healthcare deployment starts with what Eagle Eye must never see.

Patients with a right to privacy

Health information is special personal information under POPIA. Patient spaces are excluded or masked, so clinical care never becomes monitoring data.

A 24-hour operation

The hardest hours to supervise are the ones where cover matters most. Nights and weekends are where gaps appear, and where they cost the most.

Stock that can harm

Scheduled medicines and consumables are valuable, and dangerous in the wrong hands. A medicine room anyone can walk into is one nobody can account for.

Where care quietly breaks down.

Staff conduct and process, never clinical judgment. Each flag is reviewed by a person before anything happens.

Medicines & consumables

Access to medicine rooms and stock rooms by people not on the list, stock moved outside of rounds, trolleys left open.

Night & weekend cover

Nurses' stations left unattended, posts not handed over, staff leaving before relief arrives. The gaps that only show up at 3am.

Infection prevention & control

Hand hygiene at ward and isolation-room entry points, PPE where the protocol requires it, shown as trends your IPC team can act on.

Staff safety & conduct

Aggression at admissions, unauthorised visitors in staff areas, and a clear record that protects staff when a complaint is made against them.

Facility managers ask

Healthcare questions

All questions
Are patients monitored?

No. Eagle Eye is set up for staff areas and access points such as corridors, nurses' stations, medicine rooms, stores and entrances. Beds, consulting rooms, theatres, bathrooms and changing rooms are excluded, and any patient area that falls inside a camera's view is masked.

Does it process patients' health information?

It is designed not to. Health information is special personal information under POPIA, so the configuration keeps patient spaces out of view and records staff conduct and access, not clinical care.

Will it second-guess clinical decisions?

No. It does not assess treatment or clinical judgment. It looks at conduct and process: who accessed which room, whether a post was covered, whether hygiene steps at entry points were followed.

Is it suitable for care homes and frail care?

Yes. Night cover, medicine access and residents' safety are the same concerns at a smaller scale, and the same privacy rules apply to residents' rooms.
