What never changes

Different floors. The same safeguards.

Whatever your industry, the rules that protect your people are not configurable. They come built in.

  • A person reviews every flag

    Software never decides. Nothing is actioned until someone has looked at it.

  • Only what the purpose needs

    Data minimisation, in the words of POPIA: adequate, relevant and not excessive.

  • Access that leaves a trail

    Records are opened by named people only, and every opening is logged.

  • Everyone is told

    Employees know what is monitored and why. It never runs in secret.

  • Private spaces stay private

    Toilets, change rooms and patient areas are never part of it.
