Fatigue or misconduct?

The roster is evidence too.

An officer asleep at 03:00 might be breaking post orders. They might also be on a schedule that breaks the rules on rest. Eagle Eye shows you the pattern, so the decision you make is the right one, and it holds up if it is challenged.

BCEA · Sections 15 and 17

What the law says about night shifts

Night work means work after 18:00 and before 06:00. It needs the employee's agreement, an allowance or fewer working hours, and transport between home and work at the start and end of the shift.

An employer who regularly needs someone to work between 23:00 and 06:00 must tell them, in a language they understand, about the health and safety hazards of that work. Every employee is also entitled to a daily rest period of 12 consecutive hours and a weekly rest period of 36.

Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997, sections 15 and 17. A bargaining council agreement for your sector may vary these conditions where it applies.

Security managers ask

Security questions

All questions
Does SIG Eagle Eye replace our control room?

No. Your controllers still run the site. Eagle Eye adds a consistent, classified record of what happened at each post, and every flag is reviewed by a person, so controllers spend less time scrubbing through footage and more time responding.

Can it show that our patrols were actually walked?

Where the route is covered by cameras, the record shows whether it was walked, when, and how long it took. What can be linked to your existing patrol or guard-tour system is scoped at the site assessment.

We guard our clients' sites, not our own. Can we still use it?

Yes, with the client's agreement. Notices, who may access records and how long they are kept are agreed in writing with the site owner before anything goes live.

Will our officers know about it?

They must. Transparent notification is one of the built-in safeguards. Officers who do the job properly usually welcome a record that backs them up. Our page for employees explains it in plain language.
