Privacy policy

Your information, handled the way we'd advise.

We help employers handle personal information properly, so we hold ourselves to the same standard. This policy explains what we collect through this website and your enquiries, why, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Who we are

SIG Eagle Eye provides proactive labour intelligence and compliance services to employers in South Africa. For personal information collected through this website and the enquiries you send us, SIG Eagle Eye is the responsible party under POPIA.

You can reach us, and our Information Officer, at info@sigeagleeye.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in our contact form or email us, we collect what you choose to share: your name, email address and phone number, the industry page you came from, and anything you write in an email to us.

Information your browser sends. Like any website, our hosting provider records technical details when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. These server logs are used to keep the website running and secure.

Cookies. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites. Our fonts are served from our own website, so no third-party font service sees your visit. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Translation. The website loads nothing from Google unless you choose a language other than English. If you do, the page is translated by Google Translate: its text goes to Google, and a cookie remembers your language until you switch back to English.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and call you back at the time you choose
  • To assess your site and carry out your installation
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you
  • To keep this website working and secure

We process this information with your consent, which you give when you submit the form; because it is needed to take steps you have asked for before a contract is concluded; or for our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business, as section 11 of POPIA allows. We do not use it for anything unrelated, and we never sell it.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the website and our email, and only so they can do that work for us. They act as operators under POPIA and must keep the information confidential and secure.

Some providers may store information outside South Africa. Where they do, we make sure it is protected by laws, binding corporate rules or agreements that give it an adequate level of protection, as section 72 of POPIA requires. We may also disclose information where the law obliges us to.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or for as long as the law requires. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted once they are no longer needed. Records are then destroyed or de-identified so they cannot be reconstructed.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including restricted access and encrypted connections, to protect personal information against loss, damage and unauthorised access, as section 19 of POPIA requires. If we ever have reasonable grounds to believe your information has been accessed by someone without authority, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you, and for a copy of it (section 23)
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete or misleading (section 24)
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (section 11)
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before
  • Complain to the Information Regulator (section 74)

To exercise these rights, email info@sigeagleeye.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. You can contact the Information Regulator through inforegulator.org.za or at POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

8. When we provide the Eagle Eye service

This policy covers our website and your enquiries. When an employer uses the SIG Eagle Eye service at its workplace, the employer is generally the responsible party for its employees' personal information, and SIG Eagle Eye processes that information on the employer's behalf under a written agreement, as sections 20 and 21 of POPIA require.

If you work at a site that uses Eagle Eye and have a question about information held about you, please contact your employer's Information Officer first. Our page for employees explains your rights in plain language.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. This website is not directed at children.

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Email info@sigeagleeye.com.
